# Brand marks

- `mullvad-wordmark.png`: the Mullvad VPN wordmark (negative), from
  https://mullvad.net/en/press, trimmed to its ink.
- `mullvad-badge.png`: the round colour Mullvad VPN logo (positive), from
  https://mullvad.net/en/press.
- `obscura-wordmark.png`: the Obscura wordmark,
  `obscura-ui/src/res/obscura-wordmark.svg` in the Obscura client
  (github.com/Sovereign-Engineering/obscuravpn-client), filled white and
  trimmed to its ink.

The wordmarks are white on transparent and are painted tinted, so the shell
draws them in the theme's text colour.
